I've done a bit of a deep dive on potential bottom signals on this chart, of which I think there are many, but for now just check out how it looks compared to SPY. OTHERS (smaller cap cryptos) priced in BTC has been surprisingly resilient while the SPY falls. Take the three weeks of price action after the blue line as the starkest example:

Right now my base case is weeks and maybe months of ugly forced selling driven by the conflict in Iran, so I'm not actively taking out new positions in ALTs. I prefer to view this as a prelude to the direction of the market after forced selling is done and emergency printing commences. It helps a lot that if AI job displacement accelerates cryptos value prop is neutral to better, while stocks (which make their money from the consumer who just lost their job to AI) probably have a lot more to work through. In that world, where we have very loose monetary policy and dollars want to go somewhere, it's actually possible that stocks will be a much scarier place to put your money.

In short, ALTs are looking bottomed, cryptos value prop looks stronger in the future, and the market may be showing us this now as a sneak peak of what's to come we work through this drama.
